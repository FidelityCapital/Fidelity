Common questions

How much can I borrow?

From $50,000 up to $2 million, depending on the security available and your exit strategy.

How fast can I get funds?

For simple deals – clear security and a straightforward structure – we can fund within 48 hours of approval. More complex deals take a little longer, and we’ll tell you the timeline up front.

Can you help if the bank has said no?

Often, yes. We focus on the security and how the loan will be repaid, so credit history, tax debts or a short trading history don’t automatically rule you out.

What is collateralisation?

It means using more than one property or asset as security for a single loan. It can unlock funding when no single asset has enough equity on its own.

What fees apply?

[List establishment, legal and valuation fees.] Every cost is set out in your indicative terms before you commit.
